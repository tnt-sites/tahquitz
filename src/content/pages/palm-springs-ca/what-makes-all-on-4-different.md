    heading: "How All-on-4® is Different from Other Implant Dentures"
    subtext: |-
      - **Fewer Implants Required:** Unlike traditional dental implants that might require an implant for each missing tooth, the All-on-4® technique uses just four implants to support an entire arch of teeth. This significantly reduces the surgical procedure’s complexity and the associated recovery time.
      - **Immediate Functionality**: Traditional implant procedures involve multiple stages and a substantial healing period before the final restoration is placed. With All-on-4®, patients receive a temporary denture on the day of surgery, enabling immediate function.
      - **No Need for Bone Grafting:** Typically, dental implants require bone grafting if the patient lacks sufficient bone density. However, the All-on-4® method strategically places the implants at an angle, utilizing denser parts of the jawbone, and eliminating the need for bone grafting. Since bone grafting requires more time and expense, this significantly reduces costs and speeds up the dental restoration process.
