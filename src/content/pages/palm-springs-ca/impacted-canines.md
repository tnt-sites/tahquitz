      Canine teeth are the pointed teeth in the human mouth that are used for holding or grasping food. Impacted canines are blocked from emerging fully into the oral cavity. This happens for various reasons, such as crowding of the teeth, improper alignment of the teeth, or a baby tooth that refuses to fall out. While it is most common to see impacted canines in the maxilla (upper jaw), it can also occur in the mandible (lower jaw).

    heading: "Issues from Impacted Canines"
    subtext: |-
      Impacted canines can cause a number of dental problems including pain, infection, and misalignment of adjacent teeth. This could affect the functionality of your teeth, causing problems with chewing or premature wear. Additionally, they can also affect the appearance of your smile – an impacted canine can leave a gap between your teeth. In severe cases, impacted canines lead to jaw cysts, which cause damage to the surrounding teeth and bones.
