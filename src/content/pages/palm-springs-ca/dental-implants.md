    heading: "How Implants Work"
    subtext: |-
      Dental implants are composed of two parts – a titanium screw and a restoration tooth. The titanium screw is created for attaching to jawbone. Bone responds incredibly well to titanium, treating the metal as if it was a part of the natural bone matrix. The result is an exceptionally strong base for the restoration tooth. The replacement tooth is much like a crown, but it attaches to an implant instead of covering a restored tooth. Because of their strength, two dental implants can support multiple artificial teeth. Therefore, you don’t need an implant to replace every single lost tooth.
