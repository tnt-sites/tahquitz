    subtext: |-
      Dental veneers are thin coverings that cosmetically repair imperfections to your teeth. They improve your smile without the need for crowns or braces.

You have a choice in materials to use for your veneers, such as porcelain, resin, or a metal alloy. Careful measurements and digital imaging allow a dental lab to create either standard veneers or ultra-thin veneers specifically designed for your teeth.
