    heading: "Dental Implants and Your Appearance"
    subtext: |-
      A gap between teeth can be fixed with a bridge or a partial denture. While that preserves a line of teeth for you to smile and chew, it unfortunately does not preserve the jawbone underneath. That can only be accomplished by creating a new “root” to replace the original — and that requires dental implants.

      This is the case because whenever a tooth is lost, the underlying bone no longer receives stimulation from chewing. Without stimulation, the calcium and minerals in the jaw are reabsorbed and sent elsewhere. The result is a gap or depression in the jaw that alters the appearance of the face and puts nearby teeth at risk of failure.

      Dental implants solve this problem by taking the place of the original root. The pressure applied to the crown while you chew stimulates the jawbone underneath. The jaw retains its strength and its shape, and that means you continue to look young and avoid typical signs of aging that strike the mouth and jawline with the loss of teeth.

Dental implants suffer from none of the limitations of other dental appliances.
