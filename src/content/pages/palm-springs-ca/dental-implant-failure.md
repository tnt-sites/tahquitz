    heading: "Reasons for Dental Implant Failure:"
    subtext: |-
      - **Infection**: Called peri-implantitis, this is an inflammation of the bone and gum around the dental implant, usually due to bacterial infection. This can result from poor post-operative oral hygiene.
      - **Insufficient Bone Support**: If there’s inadequate bone volume or density to support the dental implant, there is a risk it might not fuse properly.
      - **Medical Conditions**: Conditions like diabetes or autoimmune disorders can hinder healing, reducing the chances of successful anchoring of the dental implant.
      - **Smoking**: Tobacco reduces blood flow to the gums, impairing the healing process and increasing the risk of dental implant failure.
      - **Bruxism**: People who clench or grind their teeth can exert excessive force on the dental implant, leading to failure.
      - **Allergic Reaction**: Though rare, some people are allergic to the alloys used in dental implants, causing inflammation and rejection.
      - **Trauma**: An accident or injury can damage a dental implant, especially if it hasn’t fully integrated with the jawbone.

    heading: "What Happens When a Dental Implant Fails?"
    subtext: |-
      The first step in addressing a failing dental implant is usually its removal. This is typically a straightforward procedure, but the patient will need time to heal before deciding on the next steps.

      Next is determining the specific reason for the failure, which dictates the treatment plan. If there is an infection, antibiotics and other treatments are prescribed. If bone loss was the issue, bone grafts are considered.

      After addressing the underlying issue and allowing ample healing time, a new dental implant can often be placed. Depending on the situation, this might be done immediately after removing the failed dental implant or after a waiting period. If re-implantation isn’t reasonable, other tooth replacement options, such as bridges or dentures, are discussed.

      Once the corrective procedure is completed, we recommend regular check-ups to ensure the success of the dental implant or alternative treatment.

      While the risk of dental implant failure is low, it’s essential to be informed of possible issues and continue to maintain good oral hygiene habits. Regular visits to Tahquitz Dental Group in Palm Springs both before and after implant placement significantly reduce the chances of complications. Please contact us with any questions or concerns you may have!
