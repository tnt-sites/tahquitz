    subtext: |-
      If immediate access to a dentist is not possible, here are some suggestions to help alleviate toothache pain:

      - Gently rinse your mouth with warm water.
      - Use dental floss to carefully remove any particles or plaque lodged between your teeth.
      - Apply a cold compress to the affected area by gently pressing it against your cheek.
      - Switch to toothpaste specifically designed for sensitive teeth and always use a soft-bristled toothbrush.
      - Take over-the-counter pain relievers, as directed.

      Please remember that the suggestions above only offer temporary relief. It is important to get professional dental care as soon as possible. If you have a tendency to grind your teeth, consult with our Palm Springs dentist about a custom nightguard.
