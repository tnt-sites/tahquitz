    subtext: |-
      There are many benefits to having your teeth professionally whitened by your dental provider. Here are the most common advantages:

      - **Customization**: Dental professionals can customize the strength and type of whitening agent based on individual needs and tooth conditions. This ensures optimal results while minimizing potential side effects.
      - **Stronger Agents**: Dental professionals have access to stronger bleaching agents than those found in OTC kits, often leading to faster and more effective results.
      - **Safety**: Application is more controlled under a dentist’s supervision, ensuring that the gums and other soft tissues are protected from the bleaching agents. This minimizes soft tissue irritation or harm.
      - **Uniform Results**: Professional applications tend to give more even results compared to OTC kits, which sometimes cause patchy or uneven whitening.
      - **Results Last Longer**: Professional teeth whitening treatments often last longer than OTC options, though regular touch-ups might still be needed with both procedures.

    heading: "The Advanced Teeth Whitening Process"
    subtext: |-
      Here are the steps that Tahquitz Dental Group takes to ensure that your professional teeth whitening experience is an excellent one.

      - **First** **is** **the consultation and examination**. A comprehensive examination determines the suitability of the teeth for whitening. This includes identifying the cause of any discoloration, examining for cavities, and ensuring gum health.
      - **Next is the dental cleaning**. It is important to thoroughly clean the teeth and remove any plaque or tartar, since they negatively affect the whitening results.
      - **Once the consultation, examination, and cleaning are completed, the whitening process begins with protection.** Our dentist applies a protective barrier or gel to the gums to shield them from the bleaching agents, as these irritate soft tissues.
      - **Then comes the application of the whitening agent.** Our dental professional applies the whitening gel to the teeth. Hydrogen peroxide or carbamide peroxide are typically the active ingredients. Some whitening systems use a special light or laser to activate the whitening agent and enhance its effectiveness. If this is the type of system used in your whitening process, then the dentist shines this light on the teeth for a specified amount of time.
      - **After the designated time is up, the whitening gel is rinsed off, and the results are evaluated.** If needed, additional cycles may be performed during the same visit or in subsequent appointments to achieve the desired shade.
      - **When the procedure is complete, our dental professionals provide guidance** on how to maintain the results, including recommending specific toothpastes or giving you take-home kits for touch-ups.
