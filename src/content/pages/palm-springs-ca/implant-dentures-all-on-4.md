    anchorName: "the-all-on-4-process"
    heading: "The All-on-4® Process"
    subtext: |-
      All-on-4® uses four implants precisely placed in the upper or lower jaw to support fixed dentures. Instead of replacing your teeth individually, each set of All-on-4® replaces an arch of teeth. Your dentist at Tahquitz Dental Group determines the best location for your implants. It’s precise work — we need to know where your jawbone is strongest. The procedure includes determining what size and design of dental prosthetics will best suit your mouth. The structure of your smile is also taken into consideration, including the state of your existing teeth and gums.

      Once installed, your dentist puts your implants through a series of tests to make sure they will withstand the forces of chewing and biting. To ensure the implants fuse with your bone, your dental team provides special “healing dentures” to protect the implants. Within three months, the implants are permanently part of your upper and lower jaws, and then your dental team installs your permanent dentures to make your new smile complete!
