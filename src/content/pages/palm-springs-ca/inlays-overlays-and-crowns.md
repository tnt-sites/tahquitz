    subtext: |-
      The amount of damage experienced by your tooth, either due to trauma, accident or decay, determines which restoration is recommended:

      - **Inlays** preserve and restore while fitting into the outside of your tooth. They might be recommended to replace old fillings that have worn out.
      - **Overlays** cover much or all of the upper portion of the tooth.

      Both of these options are different than dental crowns, which cover and protect all of a tooth.
