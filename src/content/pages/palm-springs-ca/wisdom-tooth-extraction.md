    heading: "Why Remove Wisdom Teeth?"
    subtext: |-
      Your dentist recommends wisdom teeth extraction if your third molars are hampering the functionality of your mouth, or will soon do so. Impacted teeth are stuck beneath the other molars and will never erupt properly. Some wisdom teeth that can erupt really don’t have enough space in the jaw. That causes all the other teeth to become displaced or twisted. Other reasons may also lead to removing wisdom teeth. After a thorough visual and X-ray examination, your dentist at Tahquitz Dental Group discusses the state of your wisdom teeth and the likely outcomes from keeping them, so you can make an informed decision together.
