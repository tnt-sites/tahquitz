    heading: "Understanding TMJ Disorders and Their Impact"
    subtext: |-
      Temporomandibular Joint (TMJ) disorders affect the complex joint that connects your jawbone to your skull, playing a crucial role in functions like talking, chewing, and yawning. When this joint malfunctions, it can lead to a range of painful symptoms, including:

      - **Jaw Pain or Tenderness**: Persistent discomfort around the jaw.
      - **Headaches**: Frequent headaches or migraines linked to jaw tension.
      - **Facial Pain**: Aching in the facial muscles and around the ear.
      - **Difficulty Chewing**: Trouble with eating or discomfort while chewing.
      - **Jaw Locking**: Difficulty opening or closing the mouth.

      Factors contributing to TMJ disorders include jaw injuries, arthritis, or habitual teeth grinding (bruxism). Understanding these symptoms is key to finding effective relief.
