    subtext: |-
      Bruxism, or teeth grinding, typically occurs during sleep for most patients. Some individuals experience daytime bruxism due to stress or a temporomandibular disorder. Although treatment varies between individuals, the need to shield your enamel from the detrimental forces caused by jaw clenching and teeth grinding is undeniable!

      A customized mouthguard, also referred to as a “nightguard,” is an easy solution to avoid the damage caused by teeth grinding. The guard fits perfectly onto the teeth and should not noticeably alter your speech. Most patients find that wearing the mouthguard regularly is sufficient to stop the bruxing episodes that cause morning jaw pain and headaches.
