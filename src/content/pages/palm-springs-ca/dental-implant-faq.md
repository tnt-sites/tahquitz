    heading: "How Do You Care For Dental Implants?"
    subtext: |-
      Dental implants require the same care as natural teeth. If you’ve previously lost teeth due to poor dental habits, it’s crucial to adopt a disciplined routine. Regular brushing, flossing, and semi-annual dental visits are essential. While dental implants and their attached appliances don’t decay, the surrounding gums and bones are still vulnerable. A lack of care compromises a dental implant’s stability. You can lose a dental implant just like you lose a natural tooth by ignoring dental hygiene. With proper care, a smile restored by dental implants remains beautiful and enduring.
