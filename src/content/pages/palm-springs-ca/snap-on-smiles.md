    subtext: |-
      Snap-On Smile is a wearable appliance that fits over your teeth, available in temporary or permanent forms. It’s also compatible with upper or lower teeth. Using a patented resin material, Snap-On Smile is a sturdy, thin material used to improve a person’s smile. It’s used by patients with crooked, stained, or missing teeth, or even with gaps between teeth. It’s an appliance for those who want a better smile, but need a quick alternative option to complex dental procedures.

    heading: "How Does Snap-On Smile Work?"
    subtext: |-
      It all starts by consulting with your dentist at Tahquitz Dental Group to see if your circumstances are a good fit for Snap-On Smile. If so, your dentist makes an impression or digital image of your current teeth and their alignment. They send the results to a dental lab that produces your Snap-On Smile. About three weeks later, another visit lets your dentist fit your Snap-On smile into place and make any needed adjustments. At the end of that appointment, you leave the office with an improved smile.
