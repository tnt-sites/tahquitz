    heading: "Using Professional Take-Home Whitening Kits"
    subtext: |-
      Here are the steps involved for a standard teeth whitening application:

      - **First** **is** **the consultation and examination**. Before prescribing a take-home kit, our dentist assesses your oral health and the nature of your teeth discoloration. Some types of teeth stains can only be solved with veneers.
      - **Next is taking impressions of your teeth**. If the kit includes custom trays, our dentist takes impressions of your teeth, or uses digital images to the same effect. These are sent to a dental lab where the custom trays are crafted.
      - **Once your custom dental trays are completed, you return to our office to pick up your teeth whitening kit**. At this time our dental team provides the whitening gel and offers detailed instructions on how to apply it, how long to wear the trays, and how often to use the product.
      - **Now it’s time for application!** At home, you place a specified amount of whitening gel in each tooth tray and wear them for a specific amount of time. This might range from an hour to overnight, depending on the product, your goals, and the dentist’s recommendations.
